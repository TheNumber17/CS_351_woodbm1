Description

Yakuza: Like A Dragon is the seventh iteration of the Yakuza series, developed by Ryu Ga Gotoku and released in 2020. In this JRPG classic you follow the story Kasuga Ichiban, a former Yakuza backstabbed by his boss and thrown back into outside world. Stubborn to throw away his dedication, he and a party of friends stronghead their way back to the top to find his boss again. You'll face off againt the dangerous and often absurdly odd world of Yakuza as well as play many other fun minigames such as business management, cart racing, baseball, and much more!

Current Thoughts

As I haven't completed the game yet, I don't have a full rounded review for the game yet. However, where I'm standing on it right now... this game is too damn good. I got it in June and I already have 133 hours on it. This game is dangerously addicting! The characters and the worldbuilding will fascinate you to no end, and you won't get bored of the battle system either. I highly recommend!
