Description

Plants Vs. Zombies GOTY Edition is a tower defense / strategy game developed by Popcap Games released in 2009. In this game you'll carefully plan out strategies for taking down hordes of zombies with plants! Your insane next door neighbor Crazy Dave arms you with plants that can shoot, chomp, toss, and explode the path to victory! (That is, another day with your brain uneaten).

Review

Plants vs. Zombies is a game that you may think is a simple, short adventure at first glance. You'd be entirely correct, which is why how impressive how qualititave the game is. Whenever you start a level, you will be given a long time period that shows the types of zombies that will come at you in waves. This lets you counter-pick a set of 6 plants to strike the zombie's weaknesses and defend against their strengths. After that, the level will begin and you'll have to collect sun to plant your plants while the zombies slowly cross your lawn. This is the main gameplay loop you'll be seeing throughout the game, and while its simple its also extremely effective; its fun! Fun enough to have 59 hours on the game thats 5 hours maximum. Incredible game! 9/10
