Description

The Stanley Parable: Ultra Deluxe is a narritivly driven walking simulator developed by Crows Crows Crows, released in 2022. In this game you play as Stanley, the main character of a story called "The Stanley Parable", and walk around as the narrator tells the story. You, being a real human being that can make choices however, have the opportunity to change the story, and create a new path with your only companion, Narrator. Piggybacking off of the success of the original, this rerelease adds "Ultra Deluxe" content... or, is it a sequel? Hm...

Review

Its no challange for me to say that The Stanley Parable (Ultra Deluxe or otherwise) is my favorite game of all time. Quite frankly, its a game thats bland and simple on the surface, but it works in the games favor, for every ending of Stanleys story is rather the opposite. Some would say paranormal, weird, wonderful, impossible, or strangely scary, its all in the cards for The Stanley Parable. Discover the odd story of Stanley yourself, as I highly recommend this game to anyone who wants something they've never experienced before. 10/10!
